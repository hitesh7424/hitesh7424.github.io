Who I Am

I'm a final-year Robotics and Automation Engineering student at KVCET, passionate about building systems that bridge the physical and digital world — from autonomous robots navigating real environments to PLC-controlled industrial processes.

My work spans ROS2 / Micro-ROS robot development, embedded firmware for ESP32/Raspberry Pi, industrial PLC programming (Siemens TIA Portal, Delta, Bosch Rexroth), and computer vision pipelines — all tied together with IoT networking and web-based monitoring interfaces.

I've interned at Motherson Automotive Technologies & Engineering, earned three 1st-place competition wins, and regularly participate in national-level SAE challenges.
